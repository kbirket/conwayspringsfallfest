Makers & Merchants

Vendor Registration

Spring Avenue turns into a market street from 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Register below, then mail your payment to hold your space.

Booths

10×10 — $30 each. Electricity is an additional $15, and electric spots are limited, so ask early if you need one.

Deadline

September 20, 2026. Registration and payment must both be received by then.

Bring your own

Tables, chairs, canopies, drop cords and anything else your booth needs. This is an outdoor event — plan for wind and weather.

Rules

  • No weapons, real or toy, and no fireworks.
  • Vendors are responsible for filing Kansas State Sales Tax.
  • The Conway Springs Fall Festival and the City of Conway Springs are not responsible for loss, theft or damage. Vendors accept responsibility for their own property.

Register

Fill this out and we'll follow up with payment details and your booth assignment.

Payment

Your space isn't held until payment is received. Make checks payable to the Conway Springs Fall Festival and mail to the address below by September 20.
