Makers & Merchants

Arts & Crafts Fair

Spring Avenue turns into a market street from 9:00 a.m. to 4:30 p.m. Spaces are assigned in the order applications arrive.

What you get

A booth space and your business listed on the festival map. Bring your own tent, tables and weights — it gets breezy.

How to claim one

Send in the application with your fee. Spaces are limited and usually fill before the deadline.
