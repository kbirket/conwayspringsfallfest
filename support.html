Every Dollar Stays Here

Support Fall Fest

The festival is free to attend. Buttons and donations cover the stage, the fireworks, and the activities that stay free for kids.

Buy a button

A button supports Fall Fest, enters you in the raffle, and covers your ticket to the dinner in the park. Buy buttons and check raffle winners at the concession stand.

Good to know

Bring a lawn chair — seating is limited, especially for the live stage and the fireworks.

Chip In

Donations

A gift of any size helps. Donors are recognized on this site and in the festival program.

Donate with PayPal

Rather mail a check? Make it out to the Conway Springs Fall Festival committee.
