Saturday, September 26, 2026

Schedule of Events

Most of the day happens within a few blocks of the park. Bring a lawn chair — seating is limited.

More events are still being added. Follow the Fall Fest Facebook page for the latest.

Before the Festival

Contests run in the days leading up to Fall Fest. Winners are announced on stage during the festival.

  1. Starts
    Sept 20

    The Great Medallion Hunt

    $100 prize · within Conway Springs city limits How it worksHide details

    Rules and a first, deliberately vague clue go up Sunday, September 20 at noon. Every clue after that posts promptly at 6:00 a.m.

    • Clues appear on the Fall Fest and Red Power Nutrition Facebook pages — and in Red Power Nutrition's front window, if you'd rather not chase them online.
    • The medallion is hidden somewhere inside Conway Springs city limits.
    • Found it? Bring it to Red Power Nutrition or to April Zoglmann to claim $100 and a photo for bragging rights.

    Bring curiosity, a keen eye, and maybe a friend or two.

    Details on Facebook →

  2. Deadline
    Sept 24

    Photography Contest

    Entries to City Hall by 5 p.m. Thursday, Sept 24 Contest rulesHide rules

    Open to all ages, free to enter. Up to three photographs per person, each an original work of the photographer. Photos should reflect fall, community, or the festival theme, and must be appropriate for family audiences.

    How to submit

    • Printed format — 8×10 recommended.
    • No watermarks, logos, borders or identifying marks on the photo.
    • No AI-generated images, stock photography or digital composites. Photos must not be significantly altered.
    • Include the photographer's name, contact information, the title of the photograph, and age category as of September 1 (12 and under, 13–18, or over 18).
    • All entries must reach City Hall by 5:00 p.m. Thursday, September 24.

    Judging

    A designated panel scores entries on creativity and originality, composition and technical quality, and relevance to the theme. Judges' decisions are final.

    Awards

    First, second and third place in each of three categories — Youth, Teen and Adult. A People's Choice award is decided by public vote during the festival.

    By entering, photographers give the Fall Festival Committee permission to display their photos during the festival and use them in promotional materials, with credit.

    Details on Facebook →

  3. Deadline
    Sept 24

    Porch Decorating Contest

    Photos to csfallfest@gmail.com by 5 p.m. Thursday, Sept 24 Contest rulesHide rules

    New this year: your porch doesn't have to match the festival theme — it just has to be fall. Grab the mums, pumpkins and autumn leaves and go for it.

    How to enter

    • Free to enter. You must be located within the Conway Springs School District.
    • Email a photo of your finished porch to csfallfest@gmail.com by 5:00 p.m. Thursday, September 24.

    Judging & awards

    Judged on originality, creativity and overall fall theme. Prizes go to the top three, plus a People's Choice award depending on the number of entries. The top three porches are showcased on Facebook and announced on stage during the festival.

    Details on Facebook →
