September 26 & 27, 2026

Schedule of Events

Most of the day happens within a few blocks of the park. Bring a lawn chair — seating is limited.

Before the Festival

Contests run in the days leading up to Fall Fest. Winners are announced on stage Saturday.

  1. Starts
    Sept 20

    The Great Medallion Hunt

    $100 cash prize · within Conway Springs city limits How it worksHide details

    The first clue posts Sunday, September 20 at noon. Every clue after that goes up daily at 6:00 a.m. until the medallion is found.

    Clues appear here, on the Facebook event page, and in the window of Red Power Nutrition.

    Hosted by: Tamara and Brandon Salisbury

    Details on Facebook →

  2. Deadline
    Sept 24

    Photography Contest

    Entries to City Hall by 5 p.m. Thursday, Sept 24 Contest rulesHide rules

    Up to three photos per person. Photos should reflect autumn, fall, or the Fall Festival theme, and be appropriate for family audiences.

    Age groups: 12 and under, 13–18, and over 18. First, second and third place in each, plus a grand People's Choice award.

    All entries are displayed at the Family Life Center during Fall Festival on Saturday. Winners announced on stage.

    Details on Facebook →

  3. Deadline
    Sept 24

    Porch Decorating Contest

    Photos to csfallfest@gmail.com by 5 p.m. Thursday, Sept 24 Contest rulesHide rules

    Free to enter. The theme is simply fall — it doesn't have to match the festival theme.

    Email a photo of your decorated porch to csfallfest@gmail.com with your name, address and contact information.

    Must be a resident of the Conway Springs school district. Prizes to the top three plus People's Choice.

    Details on Facebook →

  4. Deadline
    Sept 25

    Business Decorating Contest

    Judging Friday at 3:30 p.m. Contest rulesHide rules

    All businesses on and off Main Street are invited. Decorate your windows, the space in front of your business, or both.

    Let the committee know you're entering — email csfallfest@gmail.com.

    Judged on: originality, creativity and use of theme. Prizes to the top three, announced on stage.

  5. Drop off
    Sept 26

    Lego Contest

    Family Life Center · 7:00–9:00 a.m. Saturday Contest rulesHide rules

    Free to enter. Use your own legos to build your masterpiece at home, then bring it to the Family Life Center Saturday morning.

    Can't make that window? Arrange another time with Stephanie Doffing or April Zoglmann at 316-640-4096.

    Judged on: creativity, originality and complexity. Two age groups — 12 and under, 13 and above. No need to be present to win.

    Sponsor: Gambino's Pizza
