Saturday, September 26 · 11:00 a.m.

The Fall Festival Parade

This year's theme is Renaissance: Knights, Nobles & Autumn Magic. No registration, and it's free — show up, line up, and join in.

Our 2026 Parade Marshal

We're honored to recognize Judy Hilger for the many ways she has served Conway Springs — through our community, our churches, our schools and our local organizations. Her generosity and willingness to help others have left a lasting mark on this town.

Join us in congratulating Judy as she leads the parade on Saturday morning.

Thank you, Judy, for everything you've done and continue to do for Conway Springs. This honor is well deserved.

Joining the Parade

Where to line up

High school parking lot, 607 W St Louis. Enter on St Louis Street.

When

Line-up runs 10:00 to 10:45 a.m. The parade steps off promptly at 11:00.

Judging

Begins at 10:30 a.m. Prizes awarded — be in the lot by then if you want to be judged.

Anyone can enter. Floats, bikes, wagons, ATVs, golf carts, horses — all are welcome. Costumes encouraged, and go as big as you like on the theme.

Parade Route

The route starts at the high school and runs south, then east through town, finishing at Spring Avenue.
