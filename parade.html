Saturday, September 26

The Fall Festival Parade

Starts promptly at 11:00 a.m. This year's theme is Renaissance: Knights, Nobles & Autumn Magic.

No registration necessary. Show up, line up, and join in.

Where to line up

The CSHS parking lot. Use the entrance on St. Louis Street.

When

Line-up runs 9:30 to 10:45 a.m. The parade steps off promptly at 11:00.

Judging

Optional, and it begins at 10:30 a.m. To be judged, be in the lot by then — don't be late.

Who can enter

Anyone. Bring a float, a tractor, horses, a trailer, a golf cart, a side-by-side, an ATV — or decorate a bike and walk it. Promote your business, campaign or organization while you're at it.

Costumes are encouraged. There's a prize for the entry that best captures Knights, Nobles & Autumn Magic, so go as big as you like.

Questions?

Email csfallfest@gmail.com or message us on Facebook.
