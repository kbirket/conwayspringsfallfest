Saturday at Eleven

The Grand Parade

Free to enter and open to anyone — floats, tractors, horses, clubs, classic cars, walking groups, and anything with wheels and a good idea.

Route

Runs through downtown along Spring Avenue.

Lineup

Entries stage beforehand. Check in with a parade marshal for your position number.

Best in theme

A prize goes to the entry that best captures Knights, Nobles & Autumn Magic. Costumes encouraged.
