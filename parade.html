Saturday, September 26

The Fall Festival Parade

Starts promptly at 11:00 a.m. This year's theme is Renaissance: Knights, Nobles & Autumn Magic.

No registration, and it's free. Show up, line up, and join in.

Where to line up

High school parking lot, 607 W St Louis. Enter on St Louis Street.

When

Line-up runs 10:00 to 10:45 a.m. The parade steps off promptly at 11:00.

Judging

Begins at 10:30 a.m. Prizes awarded — be in the lot by then if you want to be judged.

Parade Route

The route starts at the high school and runs south, then east through town, finishing at Spring Avenue.

Who can enter

Anyone. Floats, bikes, wagons, ATVs, golf carts, horses — all are welcome. Promote your business, campaign or organization while you're at it.

Costumes are encouraged. Go as big as you like on Knights, Nobles & Autumn Magic.

Questions?
