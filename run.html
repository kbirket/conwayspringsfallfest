Saturday, September 26 · 8:00 a.m.

Vintage Bank Kansas
2-Mile Fun Run / Walk

Open to runners, joggers and walkers of all ages. The morning starts here — then stick around for the parade at eleven.

Where

West side of Vintage Bank Kansas. Registration and start are both here.

When

Registration 7:30–7:55 a.m.
Race begins at 8:00 a.m.

Cost

$10, nonrefundable. T-shirts available while supplies last.

Prizes

Drawings immediately after the run, courtesy of Vintage Bank Kansas. Must be present to win.

Register

Register below, or bring a paper entry to Vintage Bank Kansas. You can also register the morning of the race between 7:30 and 7:55 a.m.

Paying your entry fee

Registering online doesn't pay your $10 — bring it to Vintage Bank Kansas, mail it, or pay on race morning.
