The Order of the Day

Fall Fest Events

Everything happens within about three blocks, so you can't really miss it.

  1. 9:00 AM

    Pancake feed & raising of the banners

    Community building

    Breakfast until the griddles give out, then the banners go up on Spring Avenue.

  2. 10:00 AM

    Artisan market opens

    Spring Avenue

    Woodwork, candles, textiles, jam and more from makers around the county.

  3. 11:00 AM

    Games on the green

    City park

    Sack races, ring toss and a knight's obstacle course. Free for all ages.

  4. 1:00 PM

    The grand parade

    Spring Avenue

    Floats, tractors, horses, the marching band and this year's royal court.

  5. 3:00 PM

    Live music on the main stage

    Main stage

    Sets running straight through supper.

  6. 6:00 PM

    Community feast

    City park

    Long tables and shared plates. Tickets at the gate while they last.

  7. 8:00 PM

    Fireworks over the field

    Ball field

    Bring a blanket and stay for the whole thing.

One O'Clock

The Grand Parade

Free to enter and open to anyone — floats, tractors, horses, clubs, classic cars, walking groups, and anything with wheels and a good idea.

Route

Starts at the school lot, north up Spring Avenue, finishing at the city park.

Lineup

Entries stage beginning at noon. Check in with a parade marshal for your position number.

Best in theme

Judges award a prize to the entry that best captures this year's renaissance theme.

Enter the parade

Makers & Merchants

Craft Booths

Spring Avenue turns into a market street for the day. Spaces are assigned in the order applications arrive.

What you get

A 10×10 space, your business listed on the festival map, and setup access from 7:00 a.m. Bring your own tent, tables and weights — it gets breezy.

How to claim one

Send in the application with your fee. Spaces are limited and usually fill before the deadline.

Every Dollar Stays Here

Fundraising Events

A few groups raise money during the day, and all of it goes back into Conway Springs.

Silent auction

Bidding opens at 10:00 a.m. and closes at 4:00 p.m. Donated items welcome up to the day before.

Pie auction

Homemade pies go to the highest bidder immediately after the parade. Bring cash.

Raffle drawing

Tickets sold all day at the information booth. Winners announced before the evening show.

Chip In

Donations

A gift of any size covers the stage, the fireworks and the games that stay free for kids. Donors are recognized on this page and in the festival program.
