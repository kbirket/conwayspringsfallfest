Where to Eat

Food Court · 14

Johnny's Kitchen, Funky Monkey Munchies, Muffin Top Sips & Sweets, and the U Hungry Truck.

Park Concession Stand · 11

PJ's Bakery and Cold Brew, Red Power Nutrition, Leddy Family Fundraiser, MOAB BBQ, button sales, and the evening dinner.

Elsewhere

Patti's Concessions (15), Friendship Meals at the pavilion (12), Baptist Church food at the Family Life Center (1), and the FCCLA dessert bar (30).

Good to Know

Parking

Lots at the high school and elementary school, marked P on the map, plus street parking on unmarked side streets.

Seating

Bring a lawn chair. Seating is limited at the band shell and for the fireworks.

Restrooms

Marked on the map at several points around the park.
